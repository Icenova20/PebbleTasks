📋 Google Tasks on your wrist — or stay fully offline.

PebbleTasks keeps your lists on your Pebble: open tasks, mark complete, and move between lists. Connect Google Tasks to sync with your account, or choose local-only mode so everything stays on the phone.

🔑 Google sign-in uses only the Google Tasks scope (view/edit tasks). The companion OAuth helper is open source and does not store your tokens on the server — see the repo link in settings.

✅ What you can do
📂 Browse task lists and open tasks on the watch
✔️ Mark tasks complete from your wrist
☁️ Google Tasks sync when you link Google, or 📵 offline local lists when you don’t
Readable layout on small Pebble screens
🔐 Sign-in runs in your phone’s normal browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) — Pebble’s embedded config browser is not used for OAuth; after sign-in you paste a short PEBBLETASKS1… line back into app settings (explained in-app).
