# 📋 PebbleTasks — Google Tasks on your wrist ⌚

Your to-dos, right on your Pebble. 📱➡️⌚ Open lists, tick things off, and stay organised without pulling out your phone. 🎯

---

## ✨ Getting started with Google 🔐

1. ⚙️ **Open PebbleTasks settings** on your phone and choose to connect Google Tasks.
2. 🌐 **Sign in with Google** in your normal browser (Safari, Chrome, etc.) — the same way you’d sign in anywhere else (Pebble's built in web browser is not trusted by Google!)
3. 📋 **Finish the short steps on screen** — you’ll get a simple code or line to **paste back into the app**. That’s it: your watch can now load your real Google task lists. 🎉

You will be asked to grant access ONLY to **Google Tasks** ✅ — not your email 📧, contacts 👥, or anything else. No one other than you is able to view this information. The token is not stored, all code is open source.

---

## 📝 Lists — browse and create 📂

- 👆 **Pick a list** from your lists on the watch to see its tasks.
- ➕ **Create a new list** from the app when you need a fresh place for tasks (same lists you use in Google Tasks on the web or phone). 🖥️📱

---

## ✅ Tasks — quick actions 🎮

**On the task list (the list of tasks):** 📜

- 👆 **Short press Select** on a task → mark it **complete** ✅
- ⏱️ **Press and hold Select** on a task → open a **small menu** for that row (for example **delete** 🗑️)

**When you’re looking at a single task:** 🔍

- 👆 **Short press Select** → mark it **complete** ✅
- ⏱️ **Press and hold Select** → open the **task menu**:
  - ✅ Mark complete
  - 📅 **Add a due date** (synced with Google Tasks) — *Google only supports a **date** here, not a time of day, through their side of things.* ⏰❌
  - 📌 **Add to your Pebble Timeline** with the **exact date and time** you choose — great for reminders on your watch 🔔
  - 🗑️ Delete the task

---

## 🏁 Completed tasks 🎊

There’s a place to **see tasks you’ve marked complete** on the watch. 👀

- ↩️ **Tap a completed task** if you want to **mark it not done again** and put it back in play.
- 🧹 **Clear completed** when you want a tidy list — this only affects **what the watch remembers as recently completed** here; it helps you keep the completed view neat without touching your whole Google account in odd ways.

---

## 🔒 Privacy — your account, your data 🤝

**We don’t store your Google password or secret sign-in details on our servers.** 🚫💾

A **small, open-source helper** 🧩 runs in the middle of the sign-in process so Google can hand **you** a connection code that **lives on your phone** 📲 — that’s what lets PebbleTasks talk to **your** tasks. The helper exists **only** so you can get that link-up done safely; you can read the code yourself if you like. 👀

---

## ☁️ Or stay offline 📵

Prefer not to link Google? You can use **local lists** on the watch — everything stays on your phone, no cloud. 🏠🔐
