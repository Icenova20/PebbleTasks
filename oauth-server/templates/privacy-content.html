PebbleTasks is a hobby project. This page describes how data is handled.

What we collect

  • Configuration choices you submit in the settings page (for example: local vs Google mode).
  • OAuth tokens only when you choose Google mode.

How data is used

  • To enable task syncing with Google Tasks when you opt in.
  • To return settings and tokens back to the Pebble companion flow.

Sharing and disclosure of Google user data

  • Information from your Google account that we access via OAuth and the Google Tasks API is used only to provide Google Tasks sync when you choose that mode.
  • We do not sell Google user data. We do not disclose or transfer it to third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated analytics.
  • Google user data may be processed by Google (OAuth sign-in and Tasks API calls); delivered to your phone’s Pebble companion app and watch as part of the configured flow; and handled by infrastructure providers only as needed to transmit HTTPS requests (for example hosting, DNS, and TLS).

Data storage

  • Primary token storage is intended to be on the phone companion app.
  • This server may temporarily hold OAuth payloads during sign-in handoff.

Retention and deletion of Google user data

  • This helper server does not maintain a database of users or long-lived copies of Google OAuth tokens. Tokens are processed only to complete sign-in (including refresh exchanges when you use that endpoint). OAuth state values used during sign-in are short-lived and discarded after the callback.
  • We retain Google-related processing only for the time needed to fulfill the purposes described in this policy (sign-in handoff and optional token refresh). When that processing completes, we do not keep your tokens on the server.
  • OAuth tokens and task data synced from Google may remain on your phone or watch until you remove them (for example by clearing app data or uninstalling the companion app) or switch away from Google mode.
  • You can disconnect PebbleTasks from your Google account at any time using your Google account permissions page (see also “Google account permissions” below).
  • Because we do not host a per-user data store on this server, there is typically nothing server-side for us to delete on request. For privacy questions or related requests, you may open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/shan-srini/PebbleTasks. To remove data from your devices or Google’s side, use app storage controls and Google account revocation as described above.

Third-party services

PebbleTasks depends on third-party services and software (including Google APIs, Rebble/Pebble companion behavior, hosting, DNS, and network providers). Their outages, policy changes, or platform behavior can affect functionality.

Security limitations

Reasonable efforts may be used, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use at your own risk.

Google account permissions

If you connect Google, you can revoke PebbleTasks access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Your data ownership

You retain ownership of your task data. PebbleTasks does not claim ownership of your content.

No guarantees

This is an as-is hobby project. No guarantees are made about availability, security, fitness for any purpose, or data retention.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
