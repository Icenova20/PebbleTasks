PebbleTasks is a hobby project. This page describes how data is handled, including when you connect Google.

How we use Google-related data

We use information from your Google account only to provide the functionality you asked for: viewing and editing your Google Tasks through the Pebble companion app and watch. We do not use Google user data for unrelated features (for example, we do not use it for email newsletters, marketing lists, or broad “analytics” profiles).

We do not sell your data. We may share it only with parties that are strictly necessary to deliver the service you requested—principally Google (OAuth sign-in and the Google Tasks API), your device software (Pebble companion and watch), and ordinary infrastructure providers that only see encrypted HTTPS traffic in transit (for example hosting, DNS, and TLS). Those providers do not receive your data for their own purposes beyond carrying the connection.

What Google user data we collect or process

PebbleTasks is not designed to gather a profile about you (such as your name, phone number, or mailing address) into a server-side database. When you use Google integration, the following applies:

  • OAuth tokens (access token, and refresh token when Google issues one): This project’s OAuth helper server does not store them—there is no database and tokens are not written to disk. They exist only in server memory for the active HTTP request to exchange an authorization code with Google or to refresh an access token, then are returned to your Pebble companion app, which may save them locally on your phone (for example in the app’s storage) so it alone can call Google’s APIs for Tasks.
  • Google Tasks content (for example task titles, notes, lists, and completion state): fetched from Google by the companion app and shown or updated on your devices; not uploaded to or stored on this website’s server.
  • Settings in the web UI (for example local vs Google mode): used in your browser to configure the flow; we do not maintain a per-user account or task database on the server for this.

What this server stores

We do not persist OAuth tokens, refresh tokens, or Google Tasks data on this server. The only long-lived items involved in sign-in are short-lived OAuth state values kept in memory to match the callback to your session, then discarded. For the exact behavior, see the open-source server and the note on the settings page.

How data is used

  • To enable task syncing with Google Tasks when you opt in.
  • To return settings and tokens back to the Pebble companion flow so the app can talk to Google on your device.

Sharing and disclosure of Google user data

  • Information from your Google account that we access via OAuth and the Google Tasks API is used only to provide Google Tasks sync when you choose that mode.
  • We do not sell Google user data. We do not disclose or transfer it to third parties for advertising, marketing, or unrelated analytics.
  • Google user data may be processed by Google (OAuth sign-in and Tasks API calls); delivered to your phone’s Pebble companion app and watch as part of the configured flow; and handled by infrastructure providers only as needed to transmit HTTPS requests (for example hosting, DNS, and TLS).

Data storage

  • OAuth tokens: stored only on your phone by the companion app if you use Google mode—not on this server.
  • This server: in-memory handling only during each OAuth or refresh request; no token database.

Retention and deletion of Google user data

  • This helper server does not maintain a database of users and does not persist Google OAuth tokens. Tokens are handled only in memory during each sign-in or refresh HTTP response. OAuth state values used during sign-in are short-lived and discarded after the callback.
  • Google-related processing on the server lasts only for the active request (sign-in handoff or token refresh). When the response is sent, the server does not keep your tokens.
  • OAuth tokens and task data synced from Google may remain on your phone or watch until you remove them (for example by clearing app data or uninstalling the companion app) or switch away from Google mode.
  • You can disconnect PebbleTasks from your Google account at any time using your Google account permissions page (see also “Google account permissions” below).
  • Because we do not host a per-user data store on this server, there is typically nothing server-side for us to delete on request. For privacy questions or related requests, you may open an issue on the project repository: https://github.com/shan-srini/PebbleTasks. To remove data from your devices or Google’s side, use app storage controls and Google account revocation as described above.

Third-party services

PebbleTasks depends on third-party services and software (including Google APIs, Rebble/Pebble companion behavior, hosting, DNS, and network providers). Their outages, policy changes, or platform behavior can affect functionality.

Security limitations

Reasonable efforts may be used, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. Use at your own risk.

Google account permissions

If you connect Google, you can revoke PebbleTasks access at any time from your Google account permissions page.

Your data ownership

You retain ownership of your task data. PebbleTasks does not claim ownership of your content.

No guarantees

This is an as-is hobby project. No guarantees are made about availability, security, fitness for any purpose, or data retention.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Continued use after updates means you accept the revised policy.
